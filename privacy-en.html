Nuts & Bolts Privacy Policy
Updated October 25, 2026

1. Publisher
Damir Ramilevich Ableev (Gibridq). Contact: gibrid-q-studio@yandex.ru. Android package: ru.gibridq.nutssort.

2. Local game data
No account is required. Progress, records, settings, earned currency, boosters and daily-task state are stored on the device. There is no developer backend, cloud profile, chat, real-money payment system or in-app purchases. Saves are not automatically sent to the developer.
Local data remain until Android app storage is cleared or the app is removed. Starting a new game preserves records and unlocked levels; it does not erase all app data.

3. Optional advertising
Yandex Mobile Ads SDK 8.5.0 provides optional rewarded ads. Requests follow a player action. Gameplay works without watching ads. Ads need internet access; automatic SDK initialization before an ad request is disabled.
Ad requests and delivery communicate technical data to Yandex services, including IP address, device, operating system, app and request information. Ad events include impressions, closing and reward receipt. The SDK's general configuration may use device identifiers, depending on SDK settings, Android version and available permissions.
The game passes setUserConsent(false), enables setAgeRestricted(true), disables location tracking and App Ad Analytics reporting, and removes the Android AD_ID permission. The ad SDK includes AppMetrica components; separate game analytics with its own key are not configured. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise-location access. These restrictions do not eliminate all technical processing during a network request.
The ad provider is YANDEX LLC. Its processing terms, retention periods and privacy rights are described at https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/ . According to Yandex documentation, SDK data are encrypted in transit with HTTPS/TLS. Clearing game storage does not delete data on Yandex servers.

4. Choices and deletion
You can play offline and choose not to request ads. Boosters are also available for earned game currency. Refusing an ad does not create an account or upload your save.
To erase local progress, use Android Settings → Apps → Nuts & Bolts → Storage → Clear data.
For access, correction, deletion or privacy questions, contact gibrid-q-studio@yandex.ru. Yandex data controls: https://yandex.ru/support/id/data.html . Never send passwords, keys or payment details. When contacting support, your email address and message are used to answer the request and retained while resolving it; you may request deletion by replying, subject to mandatory legal requirements.

5. Children and advertising
The game does not ask for a child's name, birth date or contacts, and has no social features. SDK restrictions apply to all players without assuming their age.

6. Updates
This policy is updated when data practices change. Read it offline in Settings → About → Privacy policy. External links open a browser and are governed by the respective site's policy.
